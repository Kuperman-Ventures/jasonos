Columbia High School — Junior College Counseling Handout
Updated September 2026

For juniors planning to apply Early Decision or Early Action next fall:

Early Decision applications are due November 1 at most private colleges on your list.
The CSS Profile is required for institutional aid at many private schools.
Request teacher recommendations by May 1 so writers have time before senior year.

If applying for merit scholarships, complete each school's scholarship form when the application opens.

Campus visit season for juniors runs March through August. Register for an information session before you go.

This handout is for current juniors. Transfer applicants should see the separate transfer packet.
